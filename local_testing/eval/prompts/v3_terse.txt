Transcribe this receipt to JSON. Output the JSON object and nothing else.

{
  "store": "merchant name, or \"\"",
  "date": "YYYY-MM-DD, or \"\"",
  "total": grand total as a number,
  "items": [{"name": "as printed", "qty": integer, "price": line total}]
}

"price" is the amount printed at the right of the row, for the whole row — not the per-unit price.

"items" holds purchased goods only. Stop at the subtotal; nothing below it is an item.

Numbers bare: no symbols, no thousands separators. Never guess a value you cannot read.
